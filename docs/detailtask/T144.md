### 3. Execution Evidence Safety

* **Fail-closed** — evidence không verify → không promote PASS (T078).
* **Integrity verified** — evidence qua integrity gate (T078).
* **Immutable id** — `evidence_id` không tái sử dụng (T001 Rule 1).
* **Evidence required** — mọi evidence có provenance chain (T001 Rule 5).
* **Deterministic** — cùng evidence + verifier → cùng verdict.
* **No parallel evidence** — dùng pipeline T135→T143 + Evidence (T001) + Integrity (T078) + Policy (T113).
