### 3. Autonomous Guardrails Safety

* **Fail-closed** — vi phạm boundary → kill switch (T068).
* **Immutable id** — `guard_id` không tái sử dụng (T001 Rule 1).
* **Evidence required** — mọi decision có provenance (T001 Rule 5).
* **Deterministic** — cùng state → cùng decision.
* **No parallel guardrails** — dùng Approval Gate (T201) + Autonomous Safety Controller (T153) + Autonomy Safety (T067) + Kill Switch (T068) + Evidence (T001).
