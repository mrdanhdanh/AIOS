### 3. Security/Replay Safety

* **Fail-closed** — replay không khớp → phát hiện (T078).
* **Sandbox only** — không chạy ngoài sandbox (T136/T040).
* **Evidence required** — mọi replay có provenance (T001 Rule 5).
* **Deterministic** — cùng evidence → cùng output.
* **No parallel harness** — dùng Execution (T135) + Sandbox (T136) + Policy (T138) + Collector (T141) + Verification (T142) + Replay (T030) + Integrity (T078).
